## How to study a case

Follow the same questions:
1. What problem are we solving?
2. Who needs the product?
3. What are the functional and non-functional requirements?
4. How should the system be designed?
5. What data must be stored?
6. What can go wrong?
7. How will we test it?
8. How will we deploy and maintain it?

**Completion goal:** Connect individual concepts into one end-to-end engineering explanation.
