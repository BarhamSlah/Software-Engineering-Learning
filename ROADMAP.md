# Simple Course Roadmap

We will learn using one small project: **Campus Help Desk**.

Students report university problems. Staff solve them. We will take this idea through the main steps of a software project.

Each lesson is short and can be read on a phone. No laptop or coding is required.

## Part 1 — Understand the work

- What is Software Engineering?
- What is the SDLC?
- What does a software engineer do?
- How does a software company work?

**Project result:** Understand why the university needs the app.

## Part 2 — Understand users and their needs

- Who will use the app?
- What is a requirement?
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- What should the first version include?

**Project result:** A simple list of what students and staff need.

## Part 3 — Plan the solution

- What is software design?
- What is a user flow?
- What are frontend and backend?
- What is an API?
- What is a database?

**Project result:** A simple picture of how the app works.

## Part 4 — Understand teamwork

- Who works in a software company?
- How do developers share changes?
- What are Git, GitHub, branches, and Pull Requests?
- What is code review?

**Project result:** Understand how a team works together.

## Part 5 — Check the product

- What is a feature?
- What is a bug?
- Why do we test?
- What is a test case?
- What makes software good?

**Project result:** A simple checklist to check our app.

## Part 6 — Release and care for the product

- What is deployment?
- What happens after release?
- What is maintenance?
- Basic security and reliability

**Project result:** Understand how the app reaches users and stays useful.

## Final goal

At the end, you should be able to explain in simple words how a software idea becomes a product:

`Problem → Needs → Plan → Build → Test → Release → Improve`

## Important

This is a learning path, not a race. We will study one small idea at a time. Advanced topics can wait until the basics are clear.
